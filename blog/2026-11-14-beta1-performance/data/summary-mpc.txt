These counters measure bytes on the prover↔verifier MPC channel, not the TLS request/response payload. Native and browser runs transfer the same volume (byte counters differ only by a few bytes). The counting code is unchanged between v0.1.0-alpha.15 and the `main` tree; see `SOURCES.md`.

The reduction is cumulative over the reworked executor/session and the OT optimizations, not a single commit.
